# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 customer purchases** across different product categories.

The objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, identify high-value customer segments, analyze product preferences, and evaluate subscription behavior to generate actionable business insights.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing and spending patterns.
- Identify high-spending customers who use discounts.
- Compare revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Understand subscriber vs. non-subscriber purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-rated and most-purchased products.
- Segment customers based on purchase history.
- Analyze revenue contribution across different age groups.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📂 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

- Imported and explored the dataset using Pandas.
- Analyzed data structure using `info()` and `describe()`.
- Identified and handled 37 missing values in the Review Rating column using category-wise median imputation.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` feature.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column after checking its relationship with `discount_applied`.

### 2. Database Integration (PostgreSQL)

- Connected Python with PostgreSQL.
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into the PostgreSQL database.
- Performed SQL queries to answer important business questions.

### 3. SQL Business Analysis

Performed analysis to answer the following business questions:

1. Revenue by Gender – Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. High-Spending Discount Users – Identified customers using discounts who spent above the average purchase amount.
3. Top 5 Products by Rating – Identified products with the highest average review ratings.
4. Shipping Type Comparison – Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.
5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers – Analyzed average spending and total revenue by subscription status.
6. Discount-Dependent Products – Identified products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. Customer Segmentation – Classified customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments.
8. Top 3 Products per Category – Identified the most purchased products within each category.
9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions – Analyzed subscription behavior among customers with more than five purchases.
10. Revenue by Age Group – Calculated revenue contribution across different age groups.

### 4. Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard to visualize key business metrics and trends.

**Dashboard Highlights:**
- Total Customers
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Review Rating
- Customer Distribution by Subscription Status
- Revenue by Product Category
- Sales by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group
- Interactive Filters for Gender, Subscription Status, Category, and Shipping Type

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- Analyzed 3,900 customer transactions across 18 columns.
- Male customers generated $157,890 in revenue, compared to $75,191 from female customers.
- Customers using Express Shipping had a higher average purchase amount ($60.48) than Standard Shipping ($58.46).
- Non-subscribers generated $170,436 in total revenue, while subscribers generated $62,645.
- Loyal customers represented the largest customer segment, with 3,116 customers.
- Young Adults contributed the highest revenue ($62,143) among the analyzed age groups.
- Hats had the highest discount rate at 50%.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits and offers to encourage more customers to subscribe.
- **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers to strengthen customer retention.
- **Review Discount Policy:** Balance promotional discounts with profitability and margin control.
- **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and express-shipping customers.
